## Plugins and addons — C2UI — Plugins & Addons (C2UI‑Pl&AD)

- Plugins and addons are additional resources of the Source engine and of C2UI.
- A plugin or addon built on third-party libraries (Valve, Garry's Mod, Steinberg and others) is distributed under those libraries' licences.
- Otherwise the C2UI‑Pl&AD licence applies. It exists within the future C2UI marketplace, which is not implemented yet: the author of a free plugin or addon keeps all rights; a paid plugin or addon pays 20 % of every sale once it passes 500 downloads.
- Until the marketplace launches, everything stays free and modifiable, crediting C2UI and the plugin's or addon's own authors.
